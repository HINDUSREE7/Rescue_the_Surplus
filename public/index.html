      🛡️ Food Safety & Guardrails Protocol

      Rigorous safety boundaries, FSSAI temperature guidelines, and legal disclaimer compliance.

      Platform Disclaimer & Good Samaritan Protection: The platform coordinates real-time logistics and does not certify food safety or endorse biological suitability. Allergens are as explicitly declared by the donor. Donations comply with standard surplus donation practices and do not constitute tax or legal advice.
      🍚 Cooked Rice & Biryani

      Safe Window: 4 hours maximum.
      Storage: Maintain hot-holding at or above 60°C or chill rapidly below 5°C.
      Pathogen Risk: Bacillus cereus spore germination in ambient starch.

      🍲 Curries, Dals & Gravies

      Safe Window: 4 hours maximum.
      Storage: Keep covered, heat-retained, avoid re-dipping utensils.
      Pathogen Risk: Rapid bacterial growth in high-moisture protein broths.

      🧁 Sweets & Dairy (Mithai)

      Safe Window: 6 hours maximum.
      Storage: Strict refrigeration (≤4°C); dairy products spoil fastest in ambient humidity.
      Pathogen Risk: Lactic acid fermentation and souring.

      🥖 Bakery & Bread

      Safe Window: 24 hours.
      Storage: Dry, pest-proof ventilated packaging.
      Pathogen Risk: Mold proliferation if moisture is trapped.

      🥗 Fresh Produce (Fruits/Veg)

      Safe Window: 48 hours.
      Storage: Cool, ventilated crates away from direct sunlight.
      Pathogen Risk: Bruising and enzymatic decay.

      📦 Packaged Foods

      Safe Window: 72 hours (subject to pack expiration).
      Storage: Sealed, intact manufacturer packaging.
      Pathogen Risk: Packaging rupture or moisture breach.
